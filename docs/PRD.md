# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Living Invites

## 1. Vision & Origin
- Luxury, interactive digital stationery for Indian celebrations.
- Initially incubated as an aesthetic digital add-on for decor clients, with the goal of evolving into a self-serve platform.

## 2. Target Personas
- **Couples (24–35):** Modern, mobile-first, high design intent.
- **Milestone & Corporate (35–45):** Store openings, business achievements.
- **Traditional Greetings (45+):** 1-click WhatsApp festival greetings.

## 3. Core Experience Principles
- 9:16 mobile canvas pinned on desktop.
- Inertial smooth scroll via Lenis (feels like turning cardstock, not scrolling a website).
- Physical tactile elements: Wax seal reveal, torn paper edges, parallax framing.
- Frictionless guest actions: Direct WhatsApp RSVP, 1-tap Google Maps, calendar export.
